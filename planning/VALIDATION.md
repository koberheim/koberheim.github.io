# Validation Plan

## Hypothesis

Beginner freshwater aquarium hobbyists have a real, recurring need to sanity-check
how many/what fish a tank can hold, the widely-taught "1 inch of fish per gallon"
rule is known to be inadequate, and a free, honest, no-signup calculator addressing
this — distributed through direct community sharing plus long-tail SEO — can attract
enough recurring visitors to produce at least $1/day in tip and (later) affiliate
revenue within a few months, without spending money or fabricating demand.

## Why this counts as "validated enough to build," not "assumed"

- The pain point (overstocking, distrust of the inch-per-gallon rule) is corroborated
  by three existing competitor tools (including the long-established AqAdvisor)
  independently choosing to solve the same problem, and by general fishkeeping-
  community discussion surfaced in research (see `ASSUMPTIONS_AND_EVIDENCE.md`).
- The niche has measurably less existing supply (~3 competitors) than every
  comparable "free calculator" niche checked (5-8+ competitors each), which is a
  real, checked data point, not a hope — though it is not an empty field, so the plan
  leans on a "simpler, modern, beginner-focused" positioning rather than "first mover."
- This is desk research, not a market test. It justifies building a *small, cheap*
  MVP — not a large investment. The actual test is what happens after launch.

## What would prove/disprove the hypothesis (post-launch)

Tracked in `REVENUE_LOG.md`, reviewed on a recurring cadence:

| Signal | Source | Where to check |
|---|---|---|
| Sessions/pageviews | GitHub Pages traffic | Repo Settings → Insights → Traffic (free, admin-only) |
| Referral sources | GitHub Pages traffic | Same |
| Tips received | Ko-fi dashboard | Human account holder checks/reports |
| Affiliate clicks/conversions | Amazon Associates (once set up) | Human account holder checks/reports |

## Decision thresholds

- **~4 weeks after launch + at least one honest community share:** if there are
  fewer than ~20 sessions/week and $0 in tips, treat the "will get organic pickup"
  part of the hypothesis as weak evidence against, and reassess — either try a second
  honest share in a different community, improve the tool based on any feedback
  received, or (if truly no interest signal at all) revisit idea #7 (invoice
  generator) or #1/#5 from the scored list as a pivot.
- **If sessions are healthy but tips are $0:** the tool has demand but the
  monetization mechanism is the bottleneck — that's when to prioritize getting the
  Amazon Associates affiliate account set up (see `NEXT_STEPS.md`) rather than
  changing the product.
- Do not conclude anything from fewer than ~7 days of data; traffic is noisy at
  small scale.

## What will NOT be done to hit these numbers

No fabricated traffic, tips, reviews, or testimonials. No paid ads (violates
minimize-upfront-cost). No spamming multiple communities with the same link. No
deceptive claims about the tool's accuracy or coverage.
